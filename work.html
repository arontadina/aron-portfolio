BalikBarangay

Post-Calamity Recovery & Rehabilitation Tracking System

Live view ↗

A Community-Based Post-Disaster Recovery Monitoring Platform

Mobile-first web appWeb developmentDeployed on Vercel

BalikBarangay is a community-based post-disaster recovery monitoring platform. It works as a tracking system for the recovery and rehabilitation of a barangay after a calamity, and it is built mobile-first so it can be used on the phones people already carry. I built it end to end, and it is live now.

App screens

Landing screenSign in

Welcome screen

Residents report damage after a calamity, get a tracking number and follow what happens next. A clear note says it is not an emergency service.

Report trackingPhotos & evidence

Track a report

Each report gets a tracking number and a status, with the purok, incident date, landmark, photo evidence and a timeline of updates.

Community viewPrivacy-first

Community recovery progress

Reports received, cases closed and breakdowns by status, concern and purok. Small groups are hidden and no names or addresses show.
